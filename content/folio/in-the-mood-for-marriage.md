Marriage is between two people. Arranged marriage? That involves two families, several opinions — and occasionally, the entire city.

An eight-episode Kannada web series from Danka Studios. Set in Bengaluru, it follows Srinivas and Mrinalini as they navigate arranged marriage, family expectations, awkward conversations, and the possibility of finding something real within it all.
