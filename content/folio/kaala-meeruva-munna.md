*Kaala Meeruva Munna* — ಕಾಲ ಮೀರುವ ಮುನ್ನ — is a unique form of Kannada spoken poetry that tells the touching story of a mother and child, exploring their emotions and experiences.

A Kannada Rajyotsava release from Danka Studios, made with a poem written and narrated by Kaushik H A.
