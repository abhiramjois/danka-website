Every filmmaker remembers their first short film.

Not because it was perfect, but because it took years to begin.

*The Makers* is a docu-drama series about friendship, chaos, self-doubt, and the courage to start. Three aspiring filmmakers, one borrowed camera, and the story of how their first short film finally happened.

This isn't a success story. This is the beginning.

From the creators of Gowri and Pressure Cooker, Danka Studios presents its third project — *The Makers*.

**Status:** official trailer is out now; the series is coming soon. Follow along on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/danka.studios/).
