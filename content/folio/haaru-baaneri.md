Haaru Baaneri is the official song from Pressure Cooker — a Kannada webseries loved by thousands. It began as Shashank's private voice: the feeling of breaking out of a box and choosing his own sky. The lyric echoes that flight — simple words, big heart. The composition stays intimate so the emotion can breathe.

This track is for everyone who has ever wanted to step out, break free, and soar. Produced by Danka Studios | Label: Danka Music | In collaboration with BNMIT.
