Set in a typical middle-class home, Pressure Cooker is the story of a boy stuck between pressure and passion — between expectations and identity. A slice of life. A spark of rebellion.

A Kannada webseries by Danka Studios, in association with BNMIT Studios:
- Episode 1 — Middle class family's IIT dream
- Episode 2 — Must watch for every student & parent
- Episode 3 (Finale) — Is IIT the only dream worth chasing?
- Bonus scene — Every Student's Dilemma
